# PINK HOUSE Fish Book sources

## Species and seasons

- [Cefas Technical Report 124, table 3](https://www.cefas.co.uk/publications/techrep/tech124.pdf) documents regional occurrences in the eastern English Channel.
- [Cefas Technical Report 100, sections 6.3.17–19](https://www.cefas.co.uk/publications/techrep/tech100.pdf) describes historical catches off Dover, Folkestone and Hythe.
- [Kent & Essex IFCA recreational angling reports, 2023](https://www.kentandessex-ifca.gov.uk/website-content/agenda-item-c5-recreational-angling-report-1700039682.pdf) and [2025](https://www.kentandessex-ifca.gov.uk/website-content/agenda-itm-c5-angling-report-1763458667.pdf) provide seasonal shore and port observations.
- [Defra’s English Channel fisheries management plan](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/channel-demersal-non-quota-species-fisheries-management-plan-fmp/fisheries-management-plan-for-channel-demersal-non-quota-species--3) reports autumn and winter peaks for squid, brill, red mullet and several gurnards.
- [English Channel squid population study](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/marine-science/articles/10.3389/fmars.2024.1433071/full) distinguishes European and veined squid and describes a November recruitment peak for European squid.

The game contains 50 fish species and one squid. Channel sightings represent regional biodiversity recorded in surveys. The odds, catch lengths and waiting times are gameplay settings. Displayed seasons reflect the evidence available at the regional or local level and increase in-game odds. Each species has one album entry.

## In-game weights

Shore weights favour fish repeatedly mentioned in local recreational reports, including sea bass and colder-season whiting. The 2024 and 2025 reports describe varying mackerel catches, so mackerel has a lower base weight with a summer–autumn lift. Squid receives a larger autumn and winter lift. Cod retains a cold-season chance, with a lower base weight based on the recent reports. Regional sightings use separate gameplay weights informed by species occurrence and habitat, without treating trawl counts as recreational catch rates. The undiscovered-species multiplier is 1.6. No game odds represent measured local catch probabilities.
